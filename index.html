About KIIT Koding Club

Empowering the next generation of developers, innovators, and tech leaders

Technical Workshops

Regular hands-on sessions on trending technologies like React, Node.js, Python, ML, Blockchain, and more. Learn from industry experts and senior developers.

Hackathons & Competitions

Participate in national and international Koding competitions. Our members have won prestigious hackathons like Smart India Hackathon, Google Code-in, and more.

Networking Opportunities

Connect with alumni working at top tech companies like Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and startups. Get mentorship and career guidance.

Real-world Projects

Collaborate on open-source projects, build startups, and contribute to community initiatives. Gain practical experience beyond academics.

Peer Learning

Join study groups, Koding circles, and peer-to-peer mentoring programs. Collective learning accelerates skill development.

Career Support

Resume reviews, interview preparation, internship opportunities, and placement guidance specifically for tech roles.

🚀 Hackathons
